My research focuses on NextG wireless communication. I am currently working on MAC-layer scheduling algorithms for URLLC and other time-critical applications.

About.

I am a Ph.D. student in the Bradley Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Virginia Tech, advised by Professor Tom Hou.

I received my dual B.S. degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics from the Pennsylvania State University in 2022, graduating with magna cum laude honors. My current research interests include algorithms design for Next-G wireless networks, GPU-based real-time optimization, machine learning, and reinforcement learning.

Research Project.

MAC Scheduling for URLLC Industrial Automation

Designing MAC-layer scheduling algorithms for fully automated Industry 4.0 wireless factories, with an emphasis on URLLC and time-critical traffic.
